## Nightly regression

The scheduled full-suite run. Routine, predictable and never urgent — it has all night.

## Pre-merge check

Somebody is waiting on this to land a change. Worth being quick about, not worth the
maximum: a pull request is not an outage.

## Production incident

An engineer is bisecting a live outage and needs the suite cut up small. Worth the
maximum and worth the money, if `incident` names the outage.
